Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to your questions instantly

How do I book a service?
Go to Services → Select service → Choose date & confirm booking.
Can I cancel or reschedule booking?
Yes, go to My Bookings and select Cancel/Reschedule option.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept UPI, Debit/Credit cards, and Wallet payments.
When will I get my refund?
Refunds are processed within 3–5 working days.
How do I update my profile?
Go to Profile → Edit Profile → Save changes.
Is my data secure?
Yes, we use secure encryption to protect your data.
How can I contact support?
Use live chat or contact support page.
Do you provide 24/7 support?
Yes, our support team is available 24/7.
